**FMCG Sales Report Dashboard**
**📌 Project Overview**

The FMCG Sales Report Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance across multiple dimensions, including regions, channels, brands, product categories, and pack types. It provides a clear view of key performance indicators (KPIs) and helps businesses make data-driven decisions related to sales, promotions, and inventory management.

**🎯 Objective**

The objective of this project is to transform raw FMCG sales data into meaningful insights using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard enables users to:

Monitor overall sales performance
Track revenue and units sold
Analyze regional and channel-wise contribution
Evaluate promotion effectiveness
Identify top-performing categories and brands
Monitor stock availability and delivery quantity

**📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
Total Revenue: $19,951,301
Units Sold: 3,799,824
Delivered Quantity: 34,209,150
Stock Available: 30,081,931
Average Price per Unit: $5.25

**📈 Dashboard Features**
Revenue & Orders Trend by Month – Tracks monthly revenue and units sold from 2022 to 2024.
Category Performance Analysis – Compares revenue and sales volume across categories such as Juice, Milk, ReadyMeal, SnackBar, and Yogurt.
Brand Performance Analysis – Highlights top-performing brands based on revenue and units sold.
Revenue by Region – Shows contribution from PL-Central, PL-North, and PL-South.
Channel Mix Analysis – Compares Retail, E-commerce, and Discount channels.
Promotion vs No Promotion – Evaluates the impact of promotional campaigns on revenue.
Interactive Slicers – Filters by Region, Channel, Year, and Pack Type.

**🔍 Key Business Insights**
Revenue distribution is almost equal across all three regions, indicating a balanced market presence.
Retail contributes slightly more revenue than E-commerce and Discount channels.
Promotions have a significant impact on monthly revenue growth.
Product categories such as Milk and SnackBar generate strong sales performance.
The dashboard helps identify stock availability and delivery efficiency to support inventory planning.
